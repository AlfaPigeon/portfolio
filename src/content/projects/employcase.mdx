Employcase is a SaaS talent engagement and HR interview platform designed to modernize candidate assessment workflows through simulation-based tooling and AI-driven candidate evaluation.

## System Architecture

- **AI-Assisted Interviews**: Integrated custom AI evaluation models and automated screening pipelines to evaluate technical competency and communication skills.
- **Simulation Tooling**: Interactive simulation features that mirror real-world scenario tests for candidate skill assessment.
- **Full-Stack SaaS**: Built with Next.js, Node.js, Python, and PostgreSQL with a focus on candidate experience and HR team productivity.
