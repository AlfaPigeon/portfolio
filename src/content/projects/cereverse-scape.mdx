## Technical Architecture & Core Capabilities

- **Multiplayer Architecture**: Engineered real-time multiplayer networking and state synchronization for interactive virtual worlds.
- **Simulation Mechanics**: Designed scalable 3D physics loops, player interactions, and avatar movement controls in C#.
- **Systems Mastery**: Demonstrates capability in designing complex, low-latency multiplayer gaming and simulation systems from scratch prior to AI coding assistants.
