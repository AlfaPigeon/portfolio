This project was created in May 2025 for the Turkish General Directorate of State Airports Authority (DHMI). I built a simple airplane game for children to enjoy at technology fairs, and it is still being used in public events.

## Project Scope

- **Public-Facing Interaction**: Built a lightweight, engaging game experience tailored for children and fair visitors.
- **Government Deployment**: Designed for use in technology fairs and public engagement activities, with an emphasis on accessibility and easy interaction.
- **Game Experience Design**: Focused on fun mechanics, clear controls, and high usability for a broad audience.

The project demonstrates my ability to ship polished, user-friendly interactive experiences for real-world public and institutional use cases.
