Unheil is a single-player horror game project developed with a small independent team from initial concept through production and release.

## Production Highlights

- **Game Engineering**: Built core gameplay mechanics, atmosphere shaders, player controls, and dynamic interaction systems in Unity3D with C#.
- **Atmospheric Design**: Focused on sound design, lighting immersion, psychological tension, and environmental storytelling.
- **Project Execution**: Coordinated cross-disciplinary work including 3D art, level design, and technical integration over a 2-year development cycle.
